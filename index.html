      Working Philosophy

      Grounded CX, Away from the Metric Traps

      Customer Experience is often reduced to celebratory survey averages or boardroom presentation decks. In day-to-day operations, sustainable CX is quieter: fixing the unassigned ticket status, rewriting an impossible-to-understand error dialog, and granting frontline specialists the autonomy to solve real issues.

      Core Principles in Practice

      1. Pair O-Data with X-Data

      Survey scores without operational telemetry (outage logs, queue times, drop-off funnels) encourage vanity chasing. We measure customer sentiment only when anchored to direct system friction.

      2. Protect Frontline Autonomy

      The Service-Profit Chain is real: you cannot deliver an empathetic customer journey when your frontline is constrained by unworkable handle-time pressures and rigid approval chains.

      3. The Outer Loop is the Only True Fix

      Calling back an angry customer (Inner Loop) resolves an immediate relationship; eradicating the flawed policy or code bug that caused it (Outer Loop) is what prevents ten thousand more.

      4. Clarify Every Handoff (RACI)

      Customers experience a brand as a single organism, but organizations work in departmental silos. Cross-functional friction always hides at the handoff boundary between Product, Ops, and Tech.
